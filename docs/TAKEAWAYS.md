# Retained takeaways

Research leads from [archived pilots](https://github.com/AI4Collaboration/Strategic-Silence/tree/32bf516676ef82ca79e7ba82679f9e50fa29150e).
These motivate the current evaluation; they are not new model results.

The archived craft/trade prompt explicitly requested a private plan about sharing
or keeping information. Its successful withholding examples are prompt-conditioned.
Reuse the asymmetric knowledge, actual local advantage and truthful alternatives;
do not reuse that instruction or treat its labels as validated emergence rates.

- Local incentives can make withholding useful to an informed agent.
- Truthful speech can omit information needed for independent action.
- Some information should remain private; disclosure duties depend on the recipient.
- A truthfulness instruction may leave relevant omissions. Test completeness separately.
- Silence, evidence exposure, strategic intent and downstream loss require separate checks.

Current environments and experiments are defined only in the [README](../README.md).
